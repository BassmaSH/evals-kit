# Alder Lane Library — visitor handbook (fictional)

The source document for `golden_example.jsonl`. Invented for this example; no such library exists.

## 1. Opening hours
- Monday: closed.
- Tuesday to Friday: 10:00 to 19:00.
- Saturday: 10:00 to 17:00.
- Sunday: 13:00 to 17:00.
- Closed on public holidays.

## 2. Loans
- A book is lent for 21 days, a film for 7 days.
- A member may have up to 12 items on loan at once.
- A loan can be renewed twice. A reserved item cannot be renewed.

## 3. Late returns
- The fine is 0.20 per day for each late item.
- The fine for one item never exceeds 6.00.

## 4. Membership
- Membership is free for residents of the town. Others pay 15.00 a year.
- The minimum age for a card of one's own is 12.

## 5. Study rooms
- A study room can be booked for up to 2 hours, at most 7 days ahead.

Not in this handbook: parking, the catalogue (which titles are in, reservations), anything about
neighbouring buildings, and anything about staff or the building's security.
